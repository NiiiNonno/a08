A Test Article on Annotation

Web annotation lets readers attach notes to specific passages of a document without modifying it.

The W3C model describes a target by a set of selectors, such as a quote with surrounding context and a character position.

When the page changes slightly, the quote and its context are used to find the passage again by approximate matching.
